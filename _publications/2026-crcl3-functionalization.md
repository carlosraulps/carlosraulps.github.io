This research uses Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations to explore how transition-metal functionalization (Co, Fe, Ni) selectively activates chromium trichloride ($\text{CrCl}_3$) monolayers for the Hydrogen Evolution Reaction (HER).

Doping with transition metals creates spin-polarized active sites that significantly lower the Gibbs free energy of hydrogen adsorption ($|\Delta G_H|$), improving catalytic efficiency.
