My Bachelor's thesis project focuses on the ab initio investigation of the planar 2D carbon allotrope PHOTH-graphene (which features asymmetric 4-5-6-7-8 membered rings).

### Key Activities & Methodology:
*   **Density Functional Theory (DFT)** calculations using the hybrid HSE06 functional to determine the accurate electronic ground state and electronic band structures.
*   **Symmetry Breaking & Topology**: Exploring the electronic topological phase transitions and symmetry breaking within the complex structural basis.
*   **Mechanical & Catalytic Properties**: Modeling the mechanical strain coupling and analyzing the Hydrogen Evolution Reaction (HER) under uniaxial deformation to find metal-free catalytic pathways.
