## Summary

Physics student at **Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos (UNMSM)**, Peru, ranked **2nd in his class (Segundo Puesto de la Base)** with excellent academic performance (weighted average of 14.283/20 vs. historical average of 13.00, without any failures).

Active researcher at the **Computational Methods Applied to Nanomaterials Group (GMCAN - UNMSM)**, specializing in **Computational Condensed Matter Physics** and **Theoretical Physics of 2D Materials**. Experienced in advanced first-principles Density Functional Theory (DFT) calculations using **VASP** and **Siesta**, focusing on the transition-metal doping of chromium trichloride ($\text{CrCl}_3$) monolayers and magnetic anisotropy in collaboration with **Federal University of ABC (UFABC)**, Brazil. Currently developing his Bachelor's thesis on **PHOTH-graphene** electronic topology, symmetry breaking, mechanical properties, and catalytic efficiency (HER) using PBE and HSE06 functionals.

Solid theoretical background in quantum tunneling and electronic transport in 1D semiconductor heterostructures. Performed a research and study exchange at the **São Carlos Institute of Physics, University of São Paulo (IFSC-USP)**. Fluent in Spanish and Portuguese (certified **Celpe-Bras**), advanced English and Italian (B2), and basic Russian (A2).

## Research Experience

*   **Mar 2025 – Present**: **Principal Investigator - Bachelor's Thesis**
    *   *Computational Methods Applied to Nanomaterials Group (GMCAN), Faculty of Physical Sciences, UNMSM, Peru.*
    *   *Project*: First-Principles Investigation of Electronic Topology and Symmetry Breaking in the Complex Basis of PHOTH-Graphene.
    *   *Activities*: Ab initio research on the planar 2D carbon allotrope with asymmetric 4-5-6-7-8 rings (PHOTH-graphene). Confirmation of the electronic ground-state using hybrid functionals PBE and HSE06. Thermodynamic modeling of the Hydrogen Evolution Reaction (HER) under uniaxial strain, identifying metal-free catalytic mechanisms.
    *   [Download Project Proposal]({{ base_path }}/files/project-proposal.pdf) | [Download Motivation Letter]({{ base_path }}/files/motivation.pdf)

*   **Apr 2025 – Present**: **DFT Researcher in 2D Magnetic Materials**
    *   *Center for Natural and Human Sciences, Federal University of ABC (UFABC), Brazil.*
    *   *Project*: DFT Study of $\text{CrCl}_3$ Monolayers Doped with Co / Fe / Ni.
    *   *Activities*: First-principles DFT simulations using VASP and Siesta codes. Built doped supercells, geometry optimization, band structure calculations, DOS, charge density difference mapping, doping-induced magnetic anisotropy analysis, and ferro/antiferromagnetic phase transitions for spintronics and HER applications.

*   **Mar 2025 – Mar 2026**: **Theoretical Researcher in Quantum Transport**
    *   *GMCAN, Faculty of Physical Sciences, UNMSM, Peru.*
    *   *Project*: Electron Tunneling Time in Artificial Semiconductor Heterostructures under External Fields.
    *   *Activities*: Theoretical research on transit time and electronic tunneling. Analytical and numerical solutions of the time-independent Schrödinger equation for 1D potentials, computing transmission coefficients in multi-barrier systems using the Transfer Matrix Method (TMM) and projected Green's functions.

*   **Feb 2025 – Jul 2025**: **Researcher - Advanced Physics Laboratory**
